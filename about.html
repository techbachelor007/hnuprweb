History

History of Our School

Our school's founder couple are Thiru M.S.P.P. Rengasamy Nadar and Thirumathi Periyanayaki Ammal. Our school was established in the year 1973 with a humble beginning of 40 students and one teacher. The school started its journey in a thatched shed. The school was established with the aim of creating an awareness of the importance of education through English in this area.

Late Thiru. S. Krishnan, B.A., B.L., is really being considered the architect of our school. He had an optimistic thought that the rural children should not be deprived of the global chain for want of the universal language English. By his sustained effort he expanded the physical plan of this school and the enrolment of the students has been increased and made the fame of our school widespread.

Our school aims to impart quality education to mould praiseworthy character and all-round development of a child. Our school has a fine infrastructure of school building with proper ventilated classrooms. It has the Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Science Block which contains individual laboratories for Physics, Chemistry, Computer Science and Biology.

Our motto, "Education is Passport," reflects our everyday goal: to help every child access a lucrative global scenario through caring teachers, a disciplined environment and a genuine focus on each student's growth from Pre-KG through Class XII.
