Curriculum

Tamil Nadu State Board — Samacheer Kalvi

We follow the Samacheer Kalvi syllabus prescribed by the Government of Tamil Nadu, combined with activity-based and student-centred teaching methods from Primary through Higher Secondary level.

Primary

Pre-KG – Std V, building strong reading, writing and number-sense foundations.

Middle School

Std VI – VIII, widening subject exposure and study skills.

Secondary

Std IX – X, preparing students thoroughly for SSLC Board exams.

Higher Secondary

Std XI – XII, with Science, Bio and Commerce group options.
