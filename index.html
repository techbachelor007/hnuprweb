About Our School

A beacon of quality education in Nilakkottai

HNUPR Matriculation Hr Sec School is dedicated to providing a safe, supportive and inspiring environment where every child is encouraged to discover their potential. Following the Tamil Nadu State Board (Samacheer Kalvi) curriculum, we blend strong academics with co-curricular activities, discipline and values that prepare students to become responsible citizens.

  • Experienced and caring faculty
  • Activity-based, student-centred learning
  • Focus on discipline, values and life skills
  • Sports, arts and cultural activities
